Permently disable DNSSEC on NUC VM:
1.sudo mkdir -p /etc/systemd/resolved.conf.d
2.sudo tee /etc/systemd/resolved.conf.d/dns.conf >/dev/null <<EOF
[Resolve]
DNSSEC=no
EOF
3. sudo systemctl restart systemd-resolved
